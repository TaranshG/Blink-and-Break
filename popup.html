Check your reminders

Click a reminder to start your break. You can also open Blink & Break from the toolbar if you miss the banner.

Sound choices save automatically. App chimes can play even when Focus or Do Not Disturb silences banners; switch off Sound Notifications or pause breaks for quiet time.

Mac: missing banner or sound?
  1. No banner: Open Apple menu → System Settings → Notifications, select your browser (for example, Google Chrome), and enable Allow notifications. Under Alert Style, enable Desktop and choose Temporary or Persistent (older macOS: Banners or Alerts).
  2. Check Focus: Temporarily turn off Focus / Do Not Disturb in Control Center, or allow your browser in System Settings → Focus. Also check notification settings for screen sharing, mirroring, or a locked display if applicable.
  3. No chime: Enable Sound Notifications here and click Test Sound. In System Settings → Sound → Output, check your speaker/headphone selection, volume, and mute. The chime uses app audio, so “Play sound for notifications” does not control it.

Windows: missing banner or sound?
  1. No banner: Open Start → Settings → System → Notifications (Windows 10: Notifications & actions). Turn notifications on, select your browser, and enable notification banners.
  2. Check quiet mode: Temporarily turn off Do not disturb in Windows 11 or Focus assist in Windows 10, then test again.
  3. No chime: Turn on Sound Notifications above and click Test Sound. Open Settings → System → Sound and choose the speakers or headphones you want. Right-click the taskbar speaker icon → Open Volume mixer, then check that your browser is not muted and its volume is up. Click Test Sound again if the browser is not listed yet.

Microsoft: notification help ↗

Missing banner or sound?

Allow notifications for your browser in system settings and check Do Not Disturb. For chimes, enable Sound Notifications here, then check your output device, volume, mute, and browser volume in your system mixer. Keep your browser running for reminders.

Still not working?

Restart your browser, open Blink & Break → Settings, and try Test Notification and Test Sound again. Keep your browser running and your computer awake for reminders.

If the issue continues, use Report bug. Include your browser, whether you use Mac or Windows, which test failed, and the message shown above.

“Sent” means your browser accepted the notification. If you did not see or hear it, follow the steps above to check your computer’s settings.
